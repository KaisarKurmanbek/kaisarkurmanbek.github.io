Research standard

Every publication is designed to be useful to practitioners and transparent about what the evidence does—and does not—support.

  1. Use controlled datasets or properly sanitized public evidence.
  2. Document collection conditions, methodology, tools, and relevant commands.
  3. Separate observations from interpretation and validate findings across sources.
  4. State limitations, false-positive risks, and opportunities for further research.
